Information for Registered Providers

Learn how CareConnect Australia connects verified disability and aged care organisations with local participants seeking support.

1. What We Do for Providers

We receive inbound support requests from individuals and carers across Australia looking for specific disability or aged care services in their area. Rather than charging per lead or selling unverified enquiries, we maintain a structured directory built exclusively from Australian government-registered providers.

2. Service Differences: Level 1 vs. Level 2 Providers

Every Australian government-registered provider in our database is automatically included at Level 1. Upgrading to a paid subscription elevates your organisation to Level 2:

Level 1 – Free / Non-Subscriber

Standard Registered Listing

  • Automatically included from public Australian government provider registers.
  • Uses only your primary publicly listed business contact email and head-office location.
  • Receives general overflow referrals only when no Level 2 subscribed provider covers the requested location and service category.
Level 2 – Paid Subscriber

Priority Subscribed Provider

  • Priority Referral Routing: Matched first when participant requests align with your service categories and suburbs.
  • Custom Intake Routing: Add dedicated intake email addresses so referrals go straight to your admissions team.
  • Multi-Location & Service Coverage: Specify all active branch locations and detailed service types.
  • Flexible 1-month or 3-month subscription terms with no lock-in contracts.

3. How to Subscribe

To protect businesses against unauthorized changes, subscriptions follow a 3-step verification process:

  • Step 1: Visit the Subscriptions Form on our Feedback Page and enter your registered business name and applicant name.
  • Step 2: Our system verifies your business against our pre-listed government provider register and sends a confirmation link to your official registered email address.
  • Step 3: Open the confirmation link, accept the Terms & Conditions, enter your preferred referral emails, service locations, and service types, and complete your 1-month or 3-month payment.

4. Provider Terms, Conditions & Confidentiality

  • Eligibility: Only active, government-registered NDIS or Aged Care providers listed in our registry may subscribe[cite: 1]. If your registration is revoked or suspended, your Level 2 status will be terminated.
  • No Guaranteed Volume: Level 2 status grants priority routing for matching enquiries in your nominated areas, but CareConnect cannot guarantee a specific monthly number of referrals.
  • Strict Confidentiality: All participant names, contact details, and support needs forwarded to your organisation are strictly confidential[cite: 5]. You agree to use participant data solely for responding to their support enquiry in compliance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) and never sell or redistribute referral information.
  • Cancellations: You may unsubscribe at any time using Step 1 of the Subscriptions Form. Your Level 2 priority status remains active until the end of your current paid period.
