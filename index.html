Disability & Aged Care Support Referrals

New Here? How It Works

We connect Australians seeking disability or aged care services with registered providers in their area. Complete the relevant short form below and we will match your request without long phone queues or repetitive paperwork.

Why We Ask Only a Few Questions

We only collect basic details so we can quickly match you with suitable local providers. Detailed care assessments happen directly with the provider you choose.

Why There Are No Housing Options

Specialist disability accommodation (SDA) and aged care residential placements require formal government clinical assessments and vacancy registries that fall outside our referral scope.

What to Expect After Submitting

We review your location and support category against our directory of Australian government-registered providers and forward your enquiry so suitable services can contact you directly. Learn more about our process or see how providers participate below.

More Information for Users Provider Information

CareConnect Australia — ABN: 00 000 000 000

Independent disability and aged care referral matching service. We are not an emergency service or accommodation provider.

Terms & Limits of Service

Before we send your request, please review and accept our service limits:

  • Referral Matching Only: We pass your basic contact details to suitable registered providers; we do not deliver clinical or personal care directly.
  • No Housing Services: Requests involving accommodation or housing will not be processed.
  • Not an Emergency Service: If you require urgent medical or crisis support, please call 000.

Thank You — Request Received

Thank you for submitting your referral request. Please allow 5–7 business days for us to match your enquiry with registered providers in your area.
