More Information for Users

Everything you need to know about how our disability and aged care referral matching works, what our limits of service are, and alternative national support contacts.

1. What We Do

CareConnect Australia operates as an independent referral matching service for individuals and families seeking NDIS disability support or aged care services across Australia.

Instead of spending hours calling dozens of organisations to ask who has availability in your suburb, you submit one brief request form. We match your location and support category with Australian government-registered providers so they can contact you directly.

2. Terms & Conditions (Limits of Service)

  • No Direct Care Delivery: We are a referral directory and matching platform only. We do not employ support workers, nurses, or therapists, and we do not manage care plans.
  • No Housing or Accommodation: We do not process requests for Specialist Disability Accommodation (SDA), Supported Independent Living (SIL) vacancies, crisis shelter, or residential aged care beds.
  • No Endorsement Guarantee: While we check that providers appear on Australian government registers, you remain responsible for interviewing, vetting, and signing service agreements with any provider you choose.
  • Privacy & Consent: By submitting a request form, you consent to us forwarding your basic contact details and support category to relevant providers in your area.

3. What to Expect After You Submit

  • Step 1 – Quick Form Submission: Complete either the Disability or Aged Care request form on the Home Page using short, straightforward details.
  • Step 2 – Manual Review & Matching (5–7 Business Days): Our team reviews your request and matches it against registered providers operating in your location.
  • Step 3 – Provider Contact: Matched providers receive your enquiry and contact you directly via your preferred phone number or email to discuss availability and next steps.

4. Where Else You Could Go

If you prefer to search official government directories yourself, or if you need housing or urgent support outside our scope, please use the official resources listed below (copy the website address into your browser or dial the phone number directly):

National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) – Official provider finder and plan support

Phone: 1800 800 110 | Website: www.ndis.gov.au

My Aged Care – Government aged care assessments, home care packages, and residential aged care finder

Phone: 1800 200 422 | Website: www.myagedcare.gov.au

Disability Gateway – Free national information and referral service for people with disability and carers

Phone: 1800 643 787 | Website: www.disabilitygateway.gov.au

Lifeline & Emergency Services – Immediate 24/7 crisis or life-threatening emergencies

Emergency: 000 | Crisis Support: 13 11 14 | Website: www.lifeline.org.au
